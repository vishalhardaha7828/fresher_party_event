🚀 College Event Manager: Ek Nayi Shuruwat! ✨

Dosto, college ke events (Fresher, Farewell ya koi bhi Cultural Event) ko ab aur bhi aasan, smart aur digital banane ke liye yeh platform taiyar kiya gaya hai!

🎯 Yeh Website Kyun Banai Gayi Hai? (Main Uddeshya)

Iska mukhya maksad college ke sabhi students ko aapas me connect karana, naye aur pratibhashali (talented) logon ko aage lana, aur teamwork ko badhava dena hai, taaki sabhi milkar event ko shandar aur yaadgar bana sakein! 💪🔥

📋 Website ke Main Features & Details:

🔐 1. Multi-Role Login & Security:

  • Admin (Fix ID / PIN): Poori website aur data ka full control admin ke paas hota hai.
  • Branch Volunteers: Har branch (jaise ME, CS, AE, Civil) ke apne volunteers login karke apni collection aur entries manage kar sakte hain.
  • Participants / Students: Students apna phone number daal kar apna live status aur program ki queue check kar sakte hain.

💰 2. Collection & UPI QR Management:

  • Volunteers students se chanda ya collection lekar system me entry karte hain.
  • Har approved volunteer ka apna UPI QR Code profile par show hota hai, jisse online payment collect karna aur track karna behad aasan ho jata hai.
  • Admin ke paas sabhi volunteers ki collection ki live summary aur settlement ka option hota hai.

💸 3. Expenses & Kharcha Tracker:

  • Event me hone wale sabhi kharchon (Expenses) ka record bill/receipt ke sath rakha jata hai taaki transparency bani rahe.

🎤 4. Participants & Program Queue (Stage Management):

  • Solo ya Group performances ke liye participants register karte hain.
  • Live event ke dauran kaun sa program aane wala hai (Next Program), kiski turn hai, aur kiski performance poori ho chuki hai, yeh sab live update hota rehta hai.

📸 5. Gallery & Media Upload:

  • Event ki photos aur videos ko admin direct upload kar sakte hain jo sabhi ko Gallery section me dikhti hain.

📝 6. Tasks, Meetings & Feedback:

  • Volunteers aur committee ke liye tasks assign kiye ja sakte hain.
  • Meeting ke decisions aur agli meeting ki date save karne ka feature bhi available hai.

✨ Yeh Website Kyun Khas Hai?

  • Asan Bhasha (Hinglish): Isko use karna bahut aasan hai, koi bhi student ya volunteer ise bina kisi confusion ke chala sakta hai.
  • No Paperwork: Saara data digital sheet par secure rehta hai, jisse hisaab me galti hone ka koi chance nahi rehta.
  • Real-time Updates: Sabhi cheezein live sync hoti hain, jaise volunteer ki ranking aur participant ki queue position.
