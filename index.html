⚙️ Event Management Work Flow

1. Planning & Access: Admin panel ke zariye sabhi CR aur coordinators ko access diya jata hai.
2. Collection: Students aur Faculty se Cash ya UPI ke madhyam se collection entry hoti hai.
3. Expenses Tracking: Event ki kharidari aur kharche ka hisab yahan update hota hai.
4. Tasks & Programs: Stage performance aur decoration ki zimmedari task list me tay hoti hai.
